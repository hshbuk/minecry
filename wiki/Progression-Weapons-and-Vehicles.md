# Progression, Weapons & Vehicles / התקדמות, נשקים וכלי טיס

## 1. Score, Levels & Skills (`U` key)
Earn **Score (`PTS`)** and **Skill Points (`SP`)** from:
- Defeating hostile mobs (`15 × Tier` PTS; `50 × Tier` PTS for Illagers)
- Liberating Road Checkpoints (`200 × Tier` PTS + `1 SP`)
- Activating Pillager Towers (`500 × Tier` PTS + `1 SP`)
- Leveling up (`Level × 250` PTS threshold -> `+1 SP`)

### Skills (Max Rank 5 each, `1 SP` per rank)
- **Vitality**: `+2 Max Health` (1 full heart) per rank
- **Swiftness**: `+8% Movement Speed` per rank
- **Lethality**: `+1.5 Attack Damage` per rank
- **Scavenger**: `+25% Bonus Score` per rank

| **Flare Launcher** | `450 PTS` | Fires blazing incendiary flares at range |

## 3. Mounts & Aircraft
| Item | Score Cost | Controls & Features |
|---|---|---|
| **Scout Horse Whistle** | `150 PTS` | Right-click to summon or recall your armored Scout Horse |
| **Scout Plane Kit** | `600 PTS` | `W` to accelerate, Mouse Pitch / `Space` / `Ctrl` to climb/dive |
| **Recon Helicopter Kit** | `900 PTS` | True VTOL hover: `W/A/S/D` to fly, `Space` to ascend, `Ctrl` to descend |

*Tip: Sneak + Attack (`Shift + Left Click`) a Plane or Helicopter to pack it back into its Kit item.*

## 4. FarSight Caravan Merchants
Spawn automatically at liberated Road Checkpoints (or via `/farsight spawn_merchant`) and trade Emeralds for Arrows, Golden Apples, Scout Binoculars, Horse Whistles, Weapons, and Aircraft Kits.
