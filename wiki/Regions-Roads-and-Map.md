# Regions, Roads & Map / אזורים, דרכים ומפה

## 1. Region Difficulty Tiers (`★1` – `★5`)
Distance from world spawn `(0, 0)` determines the Tier of every Tower, Checkpoint, and Region:
- `0 – 700m`: **Tier I (`★1`)** — Recruit
- `700 – 1400m`: **Tier II (`★2`)** — Veteran (`+25%` mob HP, `+18%` damage)
- `1400 – 2200m`: **Tier III (`★3`)** — Hardened
- `2200 – 3200m`: **Tier IV (`★4`)** — Elite (Evokers spawn at Towers & Checkpoints)
- `3200m+`: **Tier V (`★5`)** — Apex (`+100%` mob HP, `+72%` damage)

## 2. Cinematic Region Flyover
Activating a Tower unlocks a **512-block radius region** (`1024×1024`) and launches a smooth **Far Cry flyover cutscene** visiting up to 4 real POIs in the region (Villages, Checkpoints, Temples, Caves) before returning you safely to the tower roof. Press **`SPACE`** at any time to skip.

## 3. Roads & Road Checkpoints
- **Smoothed Roads**: Terrain-following 3-wide roads connect each Tower to its Checkpoints and Villages in a loop, featuring slope clamping (max 1 block/step), stone-brick bridges with railings over water, solid embankments, and roadside lanterns.
- **Road Checkpoints**: Fortified enemy outposts guarded by Pillagers/Vindicators. Clear the guards and right-click the **Checkpoint Flag** (`farsight:checkpoint_flag`) to liberate it, earn Score + 1 Skill Point, spawn a **Caravan Merchant**, and unlock **Fast Travel**.

## 4. World Map (`M`), Filters & Fast Travel
- **Fog of War**: Unclaimed areas are covered by Fog of War (with a local 80-block radius visible around the player).
- **Filter (Click)**: Click any category in the **FILTER (CLICK)** legend on the right sidebar to hide/show that POI type across the Map, Top Compass, and 3D Light Pillars.
- **Fast Travel**: Click any claimed Tower or liberated Checkpoint on the map or sidebar, then click **Fast Travel** to teleport directly there.
